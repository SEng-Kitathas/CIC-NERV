## Observable reasoning operations

These are operational abstractions from successful outputs and CIC work, not hidden model chain-of-thought:

1. **Proposition Decomposition** — split surface claims into the lower-level propositions they require.
2. **Observation-Boundary Audit** — ask what is directly observed, what transformation produced the record,
   and what remains inferred.
3. **Missing-Layer Insertion** — insert omitted substrate/protocol/dependency/authority/transformation layers
   until a causal arrow has a mechanism.
4. **Common-Origin / Shared-Substrate Search** — apparent plurality is not causal independence.
5. **Contradiction Preservation** — mismatching evidence becomes a collection requirement, not noise to average.
6. **Competing Causal Models** — maintain materially different hypotheses that make different predictions.
7. **Maximum-Discrimination Collection** — prefer evidence/interventions that most efficiently separate live models.
8. **Authority / Guarantee Check** — identify which observation actually supports a consequential transition.
9. **Earliest Safe Leverage** — intervene at the earliest safely controllable causal edge that breaks harmful amplification.
10. **Requalification Closure** — function returning is not enough when stronger authority/guarantees were lost.
