# Fractal-Isomorphism Engineering (FIE) — Candidate Design Heuristic

**Status:** candidate mechanism-discovery / transfer heuristic; not a theorem, universal grammar, ontology, or promotion authority.  
**Canonical development metabolism:** `PROBE → DERIVE → EMBODY → VERIFY → RECURSE` (PDVER).  
**OIG/EDG relation:** non-canonical engineering quarry; first evaluation pass closed 2026-08-14.

## Purpose

FIE gives Personal CIC an explicit operational home for a recurring Rahl design instinct: when structurally
similar relations appear across different domains or across different scales, treat the recurrence as a probe
for a potentially reusable invariant, then try to break it before reuse.

The completed OIG/EDG EVAL First Pass materially narrows the earlier FIE framing. **0/10 original candidate
invariants survived unchanged as universal primitives.** FIE therefore does not carry a fixed "conserved
grammar." It carries a disciplined search process plus a bounded quarry of relations that survived pressure.

> **RECURRENCE IS A PROBE SIGNAL, NOT PROOF.**

> **A useful isomorphism must survive a breakpoint audit.**

## Native fractal heuristic

Native name: **FRACTAL RECURRENCE**.

Rejected weak form:

> The same organizational structure objectively exists at all scales.

Pressure-qualified formulation:

> **INTERVENTION-PRESERVING STRUCTURAL RECURRENCE ACROSS SCALE** — independently justified
> abstractions at different scales can sometimes preserve equivalent organizational relations under
> corresponding interventions and guarantee tests.

Failure signature:

> **FRACTAL LAUNDERING** — reusing the same vocabulary at another scale without independently
> establishing the abstraction, corresponding intervention, and preserved guarantee consequences.

## OIG / EDG separation

The research line distinguishes:

- **OIG** — candidate recurring organizational relations;
- **EDG** — rules governing what an analyst/agent is warranted to claim from a declared acquisition regime,
  evidence corpus, models, and guarantees;
- **operational CIC representations** — WorldState, semantic assertions, projections, traffic situation state,
  worker lifecycle, etc.

Core firewall:

`REFERENT != OBSERVATION != RECORD != MODEL != WARRANTED CLAIM != OPERATIONAL ACTION`

Personal CIC may embody bounded relations from the quarry. A CIC embodiment is not proof that the relation
is universal.

## Pressure-qualified quarry

### General / organizational relations

- **GC-01 Guarantee-Support Concordance** — a relied-upon guarantee must not outrun the support currently
  qualified for that guarantee.
- **GP-01 Guarantee-Directed Propagation Shaping** — shape disturbance/response propagation so required
  parent guarantees remain supportable, or contract them honestly. Local isolation is only one mechanism.
- **CS-01 Consequence-Scope Alignment** — in governed systems, enabled consequential effects should align
  with justified role/guarantee scope rather than all technically possible effects.
- **AB-01 Guarantee-Sufficient Abstraction** — a higher-level representation may discard detail only when it
  preserves/exposes the distinctions required by the guarantees/queries/interventions it claims to support.

### Lifecycle / epistemic relations

- **GR-01 Function Restoration != Guarantee Restoration** — restart/recovery does not automatically restore
  the prior guarantee profile; requalification is a distinct operation.
- **WT-01 Warrant Traceability** — retain sufficient lineage for later challenge, expiry, recomputation, fusion,
  diagnosis, recovery, or audit when the authority contract requires those operations.
- **RC-01 Representational Currency Sufficiency** — time-sensitive representations need refresh,
  revalidation, expiry, or an independent stability guarantee adequate to possible unobserved change.
- **ED-01 Confidence / Warrant Separation** — provider/model/operator confidence is not automatically
  correctness, reliability, warrant, or source independence.

These are non-canonical research results. They become project rules only when actual CIC pressure and normal
verification/promotion discipline earn them.

## PDVER application

### PROBE

- Notice recurrence or a suspected shared failure mechanism.
- Declare the actual query, scale, horizon, environment, intervention class, observables, and tolerance.
- Inspect acquisition limits, source lineage, authority, lifecycle, coupling/propagation, and guarantees.
- Search hostile counterexamples, shared ancestry, and hidden common-mode dependencies.

### DERIVE

Record source/target frames, shared relation, non-shared mechanics, threatened guarantees, corresponding
interventions, abstraction loss, expected failure signature, and breakpoint test.

### EMBODY

Transfer only the surviving relation using the target substrate's native mechanism. Do not make one substrate
cosplay another.

### VERIFY

Verify the target proposition under the target regime. Source success or donor prestige does not prove the
transplant.

### RECURSE

Only recurse to another domain/scale if the correspondence survives. Preserve failed predictions and
breakpoints as evidence.

## Null / anti-pattern-intoxication audit

Before load-bearing transfer, test at least:

- vocabulary projection;
- shared engineering ancestry;
- cherry-picking / survivorship bias;
- tautology;
- observer/frame construction;
- teleological projection;
- mechanism-equivalence error;
- neighboring-frame/scale failure;
- alternative mechanisms satisfying the same guarantee.

## Ontology restraint

The ontology foundation remains closed for breadth expansion. FIE/OIG/EDG may challenge a real semantic
ambiguity, but they do not authorize RC8 or conceptual-space completion. New semantic homes remain
pressure-triggered.

## Failure Assimilation relationship

A recurring failure class is a high-value FIE probe. Repair restores operation; Failure Assimilation preserves
the scar; FIE/OIG/EDG ask whether the scar identifies a bounded relation that predicts a useful safeguard at
another seam.
