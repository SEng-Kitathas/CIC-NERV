# 04 — CSC PROMOTION AND RECURSION SOP

## Purpose
This SOP defines how CSC promotes, blocks, or recurses changes.

CSC SHALL obey the archive promotion rule:
- embodiment required
- verification required
- contradiction pressure required

## Promotion Classes
CSC may classify a cleanup pass or subsystem as:
- `local_fix_only`
- `structurally_mixed`
- `promotion_candidate`
- `canonical_cleanup_candidate`

## Blockers
CSC SHALL block promotion when any of these are true:
- upstream required archive docs unavailable
- local CSC SOP set unavailable
- anti-pattern inversion missing
- holonic audit missing
- style cleanup improves appearance but not invariants
- self-audit of CSC fails

## Recursion Rule
After a pass, CSC SHALL ask:
1. what seam remains?
2. what contradiction remains?
3. what is still mixed?
4. what granular probe must remain because macro still depends on it?
5. what should be demoted rather than promoted?

## Self-Audit Rule
CSC SHALL run on itself.
The self-audit SHALL check:
- scanner false-positive noise
- machine-local assumptions
- doctrine drift in CSC rules
- missing SOP interlinks
- wrapper theater
- whether CSC findings themselves are boundary-explicit and typed enough

## Required Output
Promotion/recurse output SHOULD include:
- artifact or subsystem id
- current class
- blockers
- contradiction pressure
- embodiment status
- verification status
- recurse target

## Final Principle
Recurse does not mean churn.
Recurse means replaying the method under a more truthful state.
