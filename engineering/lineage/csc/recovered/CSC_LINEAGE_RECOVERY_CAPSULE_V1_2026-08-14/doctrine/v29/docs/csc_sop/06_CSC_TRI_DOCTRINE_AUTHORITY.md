## Always-on adoption rule
CSC SHALL behave as if all three are active at all times.
No pass may claim compliance while ignoring a load-bearing rule from one layer.

## Conflict handling rule
When layers appear to differ:
1. prefer the more explicit local constraint
2. do not drop the stronger evidence/control rule
3. record the tension in doctrine coverage rather than smoothing it away

- which tri-doctrine layer(s) it serves
- whether coverage is enforced, partial, or missing
- what would be required for promotion-grade coverage
